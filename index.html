About Ultraa Albums

We Redefine Wedding Albums to Give True Color That Lasts a Lifetime.

We came into existence in 2010 in photography. In 2011, we could not find suitable albums to signify our photos in true colors — binding was like a pack of playing cards, or costs were unreasonable. We took it as a challenge and decided to produce high-end handcrafted albums suitable for every photographer's package.

Founded in 2012 to Champion Photographers

Ultraa Album was founded in 2012 in Mumbai to redefine wedding albums. We have been offering top-end albums to our clients all over India and across the globe — including the United States, the United Kingdom, New Zealand, Canada, and Europe.

We also provide accessories like a variety of bespoke boxes, parent albums, and specialized imprinting while creating wedding albums. We offer four distinct themes of design to suit your taste, crafted by experienced designers well versed in LightRoom and Photoshop, backed by unlimited revisions to our clients.

Since packaging is very important for international shipping, we offer complete assurance to our international clients that albums reach them in perfect, intact condition.

Flush Mount Album

Fuji Crystal Archive Silver Halide · 1.0mm Archival Board · 100% Quality Guarantee

Keep your memories alive with our Flush Mount Albums. It’s the perfect way to cherish those special moments forever. Ultraa Albums creates all of our albums HAND BOUND. Our album pages aren’t solely relying on glue to secure them in — they are relying on bookbinding techniques that were used years ago when individual books were made by hand.

When comparing our album to a machine-made flush mount album, you can expect that the hand of a skilled artisan using superior materials will offer a more precise fit and a longer album life.

Craftsmanship Highlights

  • Panoramic Spreads: Hand-bound with Silver Halide prints and a thick white/black substrate in between, providing a crispy, straight panoramic view of the entire spread with no center gutter cut.
  • Archival Page Thickness: Thick archival board is 0.5mm–1.0mm thick. Prints are 0.5mm and final page thickness is 1.0mm as standard (1.5mm extra-thick option available) to withstand everyday viewing.
  • Fuji Crystal Archive Paper: Silver halide photo paper with luster finish for pure clean whites, accurate color reproduction, and lifetime image stability.

Panoramic Viewing Experience

Layflat Album

Pro Lab Printing · 0.5mm Slim Archival Core · Up to 50 Spreads

Our Layflat Albums are very similar to our Flush Mount Album except the substrate is thinner and lighter in weight, which allows more pages and is more economical. It is essentially a lighter Flush Mount Album.

It features stunning 2-page spreads with no bulging and no gutter at the center of the page. These books are printed on rich, high-density colors via pro lab photo printing. The great thing about Layflat is that you can print an image across a double page spread without worrying about folds interrupting your visual statement.

Ideal Use Cases

Whether it’s travel memories, your wedding, a milestone celebration, a family trip, or professional portfolios — interior designers, fashion designers, makeup artists, and wedding photographers all trust layflat books for showing their creative work.

Slip In Albums

Up to 300 Photos · Moisture-Proof Polypropylene · Ultrasonic Sealing

Most of us have loose prints lying in our homes, or old albums torn apart over years. Our inexpensive slip-in albums are the best way to hold them securely. We resort exclusively to photo-safe, dimpled surface polypropylene (moisture-proof) pockets, so that photographs last a lifetime.

We employ ultrasonic sealing machines for permanently securing plastic pockets to covers. Slip In Albums come with PVC white pockets and hold up to 300 photo capacity. Covers can be leather in any color you desire, or full wrap photo covers.
